Overview

This case study documents the digital transformation of a family-owned junk removal and demolition company in San Diego County — taking an outdated website with poor local visibility and turning it into a lead-generating asset that ranks on page 1 of Google.

The Challenge

The business had quality operations but faced a critical marketing problem. The website appeared outdated and failed to generate local search visibility.

"When someone needs a hot tub hauled off or a garage cleared out, they Google it — and if you're not on the first page, you don't exist."

Solution Implemented

The rebuild focused on three core areas:

1

Professional Presentation

A modern, mobile-optimized site designed to build homeowner trust and facilitate easy contact — clear pricing cues, prominent phone numbers, and friction-free quote requests.

2

Local SEO Strategy

Structured content addressing specific services — junk removal, demolition, hot tub removal, and hoarding cleanouts — across relevant neighborhood areas. Genuinely useful pages were prioritized over keyword stuffing.

3

Content Development

Before-and-after documentation, job walkthroughs, and video content captured on Ray-Ban Meta glasses during actual jobs — used to build site authority and reinforce real-world expertise.

Paid Search

Four Years of Google Ads

Alongside the organic SEO work, SD Hauling & Demo's Google Ads account has been actively managed since Q2 2022 — geo-targeted to San Diego County, structured around the highest-intent service queries (junk removal, hot tub haul-off, demolition, hoarding cleanouts), and continuously tuned around what actually drives phone calls.

"Paid search isn't a launch-and-leave channel. The wins come from staying in the account week after week — pruning what doesn't convert, doubling down on what does."
